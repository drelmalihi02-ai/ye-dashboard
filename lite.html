    Jabal Hadid camp. AA/Houthis say they targeted a gathering of Saudi officers at Jabal Hadid camp. No casualty report by cut-off.

  • 30 Oct · not confirmed

    Aden International Airport (seventh attack). The army spokesman says ballistic missiles hit the airport in the evening of 30 Oct, together with Maashiq palace. The government calls it the seventh attack on the airport. No AA claim by cut-off and no casualty report.

  • 24 Oct · hit, confirmed by both sides
    Coast guard HQ, interior ministry (Khor Maksar). The interior ministry says 2 ballistic missiles hit the coast guard HQ at the ministry in the afternoon. Time not stated, site approximate.
