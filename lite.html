Aden strike count

13 attacks since 1 Sep, 7 confirmed by both sides. First on 2 Oct.

  • 28 Oct 15:00 · not confirmed
    Aden International Airport (sixth attack). The transport ministry reports a second strike at 15:00. The army spokesman says 2 ballistic missiles hit the airport, injuring civilians and causing damage (number of injured not stated). The government calls it the sixth attack on the airport within days.
  • 28 Oct 13:00 · not confirmed
    Aden International Airport (fifth attack). The transport ministry says several ballistic missiles hit the airport at 13:00, the first of two strikes that day. AA/Houthis had told people to evacuate the airport, saying a Saudi military cargo plane was there.
  • 25 Oct · hit, confirmed by both sides
    Near displacement camps, Salah al-Din. The government says 2 ballistic missiles fell near displacement camps in the Salah al-Din area on the night of 25 Oct (reported 01:19 on 26 Oct). AA/Houthis say they hit Saudi-run mobilisations and arms stores there. No casualty report. Time not stated, site west of the city map.
  • 24 Oct · hit, confirmed by both sides
    Coast guard building, Tawahi port. The government says a third ballistic missile fell near the Tawahi port quay and the coast guard building, hitting a small fishing boat and injuring a fisherman. AA/Houthis say they hit the naval base in Aden. Time not stated.
  • 24 Oct · hit, confirmed by both sides
    Coast guard HQ, interior ministry (Khor Maksar). The interior ministry says 2 ballistic missiles hit the coast guard HQ at the ministry in the afternoon. The transport ministry says 2 missiles targeted Aden airport, its 4th attack this month. AA/Houthis say they hit Saudi-run mobilisations at Al-Nasr camp. No casualty report. Time not stated, site approximate.
  • 21 Oct · hit, confirmed by both sides
    Aden International Airport. The fourth ballistic missile attack on the airport, says the transport ministry. It struck after two flights left, halting two more departures and forcing an evacuation. No casualties were reported. AA/Houthis say they hit the Saudi operations room at Badr camp next to the airport. Time not stated.
  • 17 Oct 03:31 · not confirmed
    Maashiq palace, Crater (unconfirmed). One outside report says ballistic missiles struck the palace. No AA claim or government statement by cut-off. It may refer to the 15 Oct strike.
  • 16 Oct 21:50 · hit, confirmed by both sides
    Aden International Airport grounds. 2 missiles fell inside the airport grounds, the second strike on the airport in a week. Aden security says early information shows no deaths and flights were operating. AA/Houthis say they hit a Saudi command HQ and weapons stores at Badr camp next to the airport.
  • 15 Oct 22:20 · not confirmed
    New wave on the city. Explosions reported across Aden hours after the palace strike. Target unknown.
  • 15 Oct 17:30 · hit, confirmed by both sides
    Maashiq palace, Crater. Missiles and drones. The government reports guards killed and injured (combatants, not counted). AA/Houthis say they hit an operations HQ run by Saudi officers.
  • 11 Oct · not confirmed
    Aden International Airport. The government says a missile and drone attack hit the airport as a Yemenia flight from Cairo approached, forcing its diversion to Jeddah. The national investigation committee lists 11 Oct among the attacks on the airport. No AA claim. Time not stated.
  • 7 Oct 03:00 · hit, confirmed by both sides
    Aden International Airport, Khor Maksar. 2 ballistic missiles plus drones. One missile fell near the runway minutes before a civilian flight from Cairo landed. AA/Houthis say they hit Saudi military supplies at Badr camp next to the airport.
  • 2 Oct · intercepted
    Drones over Aden city. PLC forces say air defences shot down several suicide drones before they reached residential areas. AA/Houthis did not claim it.
